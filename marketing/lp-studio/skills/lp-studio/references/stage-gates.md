# Stage gates v0.2
## Brief → Blind Pain Research
- Target role/organization and situation are specific enough.
- Founder/product problem statements are not external pain evidence.
## Blind Pain Research → Market Research
- Researcher was not primed with mechanisms or preferred positioning.
- >=2 materially different externally evidenced pain clusters.
- Workarounds and counterevidence retained.
- Product fit scored only after clustering.
## Market Research → Synthesis
- Competitors, adjacent solutions and substitutes sampled.
- Competitor messaging is not pain/performance proof.
- Competitor-owned narratives identified.
## Synthesis → Positioning
- Product-derived vs externally observed pains separated.
- Candidate pains have evidence-strength and product-fit ratings.
- At least one option does not merely restate product architecture.
## Positioning → Message
- >=3 materially different problem-positioning options.
- Selected option states target, trigger, pain, workaround, promise, mechanism, evidence, risk.
## Message → Design
- >=3 persuasion architectures; each section has a persuasion job.
## Design → Prototype
- >=3 visual directions differ by visual anchor, not color.
## Prototype → QA
- Evidence, conversion, visual, technical QA completed.
- Unsupported claims absent/placeholders.
- Upstream changes invalidate downstream work.


## v0.3 — Message Strategy → Copy Development
- At least 3 message strategies differ by persuasion starting point, not tone.
- Strategy types are selected from Pain / Job / Outcome / Mechanism / Category-reframe / Alternative according to evidence.
- One strategy is selected before copywriting begins.
- Information requirements for the first viewport are explicit.

## Copy Development → Visual
- Product Truth, Customer Value and Plain Message exist before persuasive copy.
- Compression losses are recorded; no selected copy has `core_meaning_loss: high`.
- Reader Prior Knowledge is assessed.
- Hero is designed as a Message Unit, not headline-only.
- A blind comprehension auditor sees only the Hero unit.
- At least 3 of Who / What product / What user does / Benefit materially match intended meaning.
- No critical false inference and no unsupported claim.
- Failure returns to the appropriate Copy Ladder rung rather than cosmetic rewriting.
