## Inference (kept separate from observation)
- The single, repeated "Talk to sales" ask (no self-serve signup, no form) suggests Guru's ICP for this LP is mid-market/enterprise buyers who expect a sales-assisted evaluation, not PLG self-service.
- Leading with an AI-trust/accuracy problem ("confidently wrong AI") rather than a knowledge-management capability claim suggests the LP is positioned to intercept "we're rolling out AI and worried about hallucinations" buying conversations, not classic "wiki/knowledge base" search.
- The docked AI SDR chat widget functioning as a second, lower-friction conversion surface alongside "Talk to sales" implies the team is testing conversational/agentic lead capture as a parallel path to the traditional demo-request CTA.
