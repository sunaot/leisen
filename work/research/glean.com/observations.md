## Visual
- R-glean-30 Type: H1 font family "Polysans Neutral", Arial, sans-serif at 56px / weight 400 / black (rgb(0,0,0)). Body/UI text runs in the same custom family group (Polysans Slim / Polysans Neutral Mono appear as secondary weights) predominantly at 16px (most frequent text size by far), with 20px/40px/36px/24px used for sub-headings and stat numbers. Entirely sans-serif (a proprietary grotesk-style face called "Polysans"); no serif usage observed.
- R-glean-31 Colour: Background base is a warm off-white/cream, rgb(251,246,244) (by far the most-used background), alternating with pure white (rgb(255,255,255)) sections and full-black (rgb(0,0,0)) sections used as deliberate dark bands (nav/announcement bar, "Work AI Institute" section, footer). Accent colors: an indigo/blue rgb(52,59,237) for the primary CTA button, and a neon yellow-green rgb(216,253,73) used sparingly as a highlight. Gradients are extensive but are photographic/3D-render style (glossy chrome ribbons in pink–purple–blue, blue–white, and green–orange–blue–purple combinations) rather than flat CSS color gradients. No user-facing dark-mode toggle (styles.json darkMode: false) — the black sections are a fixed design choice, not a theme state.
- R-glean-32 Density: Uneven — hero and most narrative sections are spacious (large type, generous cream whitespace), while the two aggregate-stat blocks (section 7 and section 9's stat wall) and the footer are visually dense, packing many small stat tiles or link columns edge-to-edge.
- R-glean-33 Imagery: Two distinct imagery types are used side by side — (1) real-looking product UI mockups presented as clean white cards (search bar with app-connector chips, an agent "Plan" checklist with progress bar, a "Builder assistant" chat + "Instructions" panel pair, a grid of connected third-party app icons) — no visible annotation call-outs and no animation (static capture); and (2) abstract, glossy 3D-rendered gradient/chrome ribbon graphics used as backgrounds and section dividers throughout. Only one real photograph appears (a blue-toned lifestyle/interior shot inside the Booking.com case-study card); otherwise no photos of people or offices.
- R-glean-34 Distinctive: The repeated glossy, iridescent 3D-chrome "ribbon/wave" graphic motif (recolored per section — pink/blue in the hero, blue/white mid-page, multicolor green-orange-blue-purple near the community block) functions as a consistent brand device in place of stock photography or flat illustration, while small embedded product-UI cards carry the concrete "this is what using Glean looks like" proof.

## Inference (kept separate from observation)
- The heavy stacking of quantified proof (token counters, two overlapping aggregate-stat walls, named customer stats, certification badges) suggests the page is built to reassure a skeptical, ROI- and risk-driven buying committee (IT/security + finance + business unit) rather than to persuade with a single emotional hook.
- No pricing, no self-serve trial/signup anywhere on the LP signals a purely sales-assisted, enterprise motion where the page's only job is to generate demo requests, not conversions to product usage.
- Two separate, only partially-consistent aggregate-outcome stat blocks (with numbers that could be read as overlapping — e.g., multiple different "% adoption" figures) hints at a componentized/modular page-assembly approach (reusable stat-block components) more than a single tightly authored numeric narrative.
- Using an abstract chrome-gradient graphic as the primary hero visual (rather than a full product screenshot) lets Glean keep brand imagery abstract/premium while still parking a small, concrete "search across your apps" UI mockup inside it — a hedge between brand feel and product proof.
