## Inference (kept separate from observation)
- The near-total absence of an on-page lead form, combined with 6+ differently-worded "talk to sales" CTAs, suggests this LP is optimized for sales-assisted enterprise deals rather than self-serve trial signup, despite a "Start a free trial" link existing (buried in a nav flyout).
- Repeating Gartner/analyst proof three times on one page implies the target buyer's stated or assumed top objection is credibility/risk-of-choice among enterprise search vendors, not price or ease of use.
- The embedded "Aria" chat widget positioned inside the hero (rather than as a bottom-corner launcher) suggests Coveo is using the LP itself as a live demo of its own conversational-AI product, doubling as proof-by-example for the "AI-Search & Conversational Discovery" claim.
