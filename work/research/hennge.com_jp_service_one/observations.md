## Inference (kept separate from observation)
- The hero's real headline lives entirely in an image (empty `<h1>` text node), so any text-based scraping/SEO or accessibility read of the page would miss the actual headline copy — suggests the design team prioritized visual polish over a crawlable/read-aloud headline.
- The mismatch between the screenshot's visible "プランと価格" CTA and its total absence from the extracted `ctas` array hints this button may be rendered via a non-standard element (e.g., a styled `<div>` with a click handler, or loaded from an iframe/widget) rather than a plain `<a>`/`<button>`, which is itself a possible technical/accessibility signal.
- The heavy reliance on quantified proof (3,800社, 100超システム, 10分の1, 5年連続1位) paired with a distinctly playful mascot visual suggests HENNGE is deliberately balancing "serious enterprise credibility" messaging against a differentiated, memorable brand character — a combination a Ring C challenger LP could contrast against (e.g., leading with plain-language capability claims instead of ranking badges, or choosing a more literal/functional hero visual over a mascot metaphor) to read as more substantive to a skeptical 情シス buyer.
