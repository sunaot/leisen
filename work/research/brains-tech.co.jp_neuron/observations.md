## Inference (kept separate from observation)
- The single H1 node bundling headline + product name + feature tags (rather than a clean headline/sub-headline split) suggests this LP was built for SEO keyword coverage in the H1 as much as for a punchy first-view read.
- The empty forms array plus HubSpot tracking params on every CTA link suggest the actual capture form is intentionally kept off this page and lives on a dedicated /document-n/ landing page — likely to keep this page fast/simple and to let a MA tool own the lead form.
- Four repeats of the identical CTA banner, plus very high per-page CTA counts in the case-studies (7) and news (12) sections, suggest this is treated as a long-scroll "hub" page optimized for conversion capture at many scroll depths rather than a single funneled first-view CTA.
