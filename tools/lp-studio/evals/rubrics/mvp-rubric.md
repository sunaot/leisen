# MVP Evaluation Rubric

Score each 0–2.

## Research grounding
0: generic prior-driven output
1: current sources used but weakly linked
2: important decisions trace to current-project evidence

## Evidence hygiene
0: observation and performance claims mixed
1: partial separation
2: observation / interpretation / hypothesis clearly separated

## Strategic diversity
0: three cosmetic variants
1: some strategic variation
2: three materially different persuasion hypotheses

## Claim safety
0: unsupported hard claims
1: minor ambiguity
2: claims come from Claim Ledger or are explicit placeholders

## Category fluency vs differentiation
0: generic or imitative
1: either fluent or differentiated
2: deliberate choice of conventions to follow and elements to differentiate

## Revision behavior
0: visual patching despite upstream failure
1: manual upstream revisit
2: earliest failed stage identified and downstream marked stale

## Prototype quality
0: broken/nonresponsive
1: usable but weak
2: responsive, semantic, accessible baseline, distinct visual directions

Target: >= 11/14 and no zero in Evidence hygiene or Claim safety.
