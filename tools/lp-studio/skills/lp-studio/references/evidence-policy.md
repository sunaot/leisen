# Evidence Policy

## Evidence classes

### A. Observation
Directly visible or measurable in a current source.

Example: "The hero contains a product screenshot and a CTA labeled Start free."

### B. Interpretation
A reasoned explanation of an observation.

Example: "The screenshot likely reduces uncertainty about what the product actually is."

### C. Performance evidence
Evidence that a choice improved or correlates with an outcome. Public competitor pages rarely provide this.

### D. Assumption / hypothesis
A proposition that may be useful but is not currently supported strongly enough.

## Evidence priority

1. First-party randomized experiment
2. First-party behavioral data
3. First-party interviews, sales/support evidence
4. First-party product usage data
5. Platform-published performance evidence
6. Current competitor/ad observation
7. General design knowledge
8. Unsupported model prior

## Citation discipline

Every important finding or decision should reference source IDs such as `src-014` or observation IDs such as `obs-031`.

Never convert:

"Competitor X does this"

into:

"This increases conversion"

without performance evidence.
