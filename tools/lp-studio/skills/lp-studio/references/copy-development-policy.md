# Copy Development Policy v0.3

## Core rule
Copy compresses established meaning; it does not invent the meaning.

## Quality objective
Prefer necessary and sufficient information at the lowest cognitive cost over slogan-like brevity.

## Anti-patterns
Reject or revise:
- abstract headlines with no concrete domain anchor when product/category familiarity is low;
- comparison words whose comparison target is omitted;
- verbs whose object/action is unknowable to a first-time reader;
- fashionable fragments that require creator context;
- established-brand information density applied to unknown products;
- three “variations” that only change tone;
- headlines graded only by the agent that wrote them.

## Copy Ladder
Product Truth → Customer Value → Plain Message → Persuasive Copy → optional Brand Copy.

The selected rung may be Plain Message or Persuasive Copy. Brand Copy is not the goal.

## Hero Message Unit
Meaning may be distributed across eyebrow, headline, subheadline, visual, CTA and supported proof. Evaluate the unit, not the headline in isolation.

## Blind comprehension
The auditor must be context-starved intentionally. Compare inferred meaning to intended information requirements.
